How about we turn any image into a 3D
model? Isn't that just dope? To get some
images, you can go to Pinterest, search
for anything interesting to you, make
the search on front face, then save the
ones you like. Now, we need to take this
image we saved and drop it on this site
which is fully free. We do this to
generate a depth map, a kind of map to
determine the distance of the surfaces
from the viewer. White is close surface
while black is far.
So open the image here. Wait for it a
few seconds and you will get this heat
map. So switch to the second option for
the depth map. We can also increase
ensemble size to max for better quality.
So do that. Hit apply and save the depth
map with right click and save image.
Next, in Blender, let's clear the scene.
Then, from the add menu, go to image
mesh plane or what used to be known as
image as plane. Pick it and choose the
image from Pinterest to import it to the
scene. We need some subs on this plane.
So, right click in edit mode and
subdivide. Might go up to 40 or 50. Once
you're done, go to the modifiers and
look for the displace one. Add it on the
plane. Then hit new and click on this
show texture icon.
Here we can open the depth map. Switch
the color space to non-color. Then back
to the displayplace modifier. Put the
coordinates on UV and adjust the
strength to fit. A subdivision surface
modifier is needed here to smooth the
shape. And you can adjust the level
based on the details. And that's it.
Hope you have fun with it. Like and sub
for more dope and drop any question in
the comments. Stay sharp. Goodbye.
